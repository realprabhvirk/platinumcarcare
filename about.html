About

What Platinum Car Care Is, And Isn't.

Platinum Car Care is a mobile detailing business operating across Brisbane. We bring the detail to you, and we would rather tell you plainly what you are getting than talk it up.

On Location, Every Time.

No workshop, no drop-off. We turn up with a fully equipped mobile setup and get to work wherever the car is, your driveway, your workplace, wherever suits you.

  • Everything needed brought to the job
  • Same careful process, every vehicle
  • Servicing Brisbane and surrounding suburbs

What We're Not

A cheap car wash. We are not a $20 rinse and go.

An automated wash. Every detail is done by hand, not run through a machine.

A business that overpromises. We do not claim a coating or treatment will do more than it actually does.

What We Focus On

Careful workmanship. Each job gets the time it actually needs.

Quality products. Safe for your paint and interior, used properly.

Realistic expectations. A straight answer about what your car needs and what a service will and won't do.

Convenience. Detailed at your home or workplace, on your schedule.
