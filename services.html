Services

Professional Detailing, Delivered To You.

Pricing is enquiry based. Every quote depends on your vehicle's size and condition, so tell us about the car and we will give you a straight answer before any work starts.

01

Exterior Wash & Wax

A full exterior wash, clay and hand wax finish that lifts road grime and restores depth in the paint. Wheels, tyres and exterior trim are cleaned and dressed as part of the job.

  • Pre-wash, foam wash and hand dry
  • Clay bar treatment and protective wax
  • Wheel, tyre and exterior trim clean
02

Interior Deep Clean

A complete interior reset. Seats, carpets and mats are vacuumed and cleaned, trim and touchpoints wiped down, and the areas most washes skip, console, door pockets and window tracks, are gone through by hand.

  • Seats, carpets and mats vacuumed and cleaned
  • Dash, console and trim wiped down
  • Glass, door pockets and window tracks
03

Paint Protection

A protection focused finishing step that helps maintain gloss, improve presentation and make ongoing upkeep easier between details.

  • Paint decontamination and correction step
  • Protective sealant application
  • Guidance on maintaining the finish at home
04

Ceramic Coating

Long-term protection, easier cleaning and enhanced gloss for a lasting finish. Ceramic coating is subject to appropriate paint preparation and vehicle condition, it is not automatically suitable for every car, and we will tell you honestly whether your vehicle is a good candidate before booking it in.

  • Full paint decontamination and preparation
  • Ceramic coating application
  • Suitability assessed on vehicle condition, no exceptions
05

Mobile Detailing

The whole point of Platinum Car Care. Every service on this page is carried out at your home or workplace, not a workshop drop-off, so you skip the trip and the waiting around.

  • Fully equipped mobile setup
  • Servicing Brisbane, Logan, Ipswich, Redlands and Moreton Bay
  • Water or power needs, if any, confirmed before your appointment
06

Maintenance Details

Regular professional upkeep between full details, so your car stays consistently presentable all year round instead of needing a big correction each time.

  • Set on a schedule that suits you
  • Lighter scope, same careful process
  • Keeps protection and presentation topped up
