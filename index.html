FAQ

Common Questions.

Do you have set prices?

Yes, for our three main packages: Regular Maintenance ($150), Mini Detail ($180) and Full Detail ($360). Paint correction and ceramic coating stay quote based since they depend on your vehicle's paint condition.

Are the prices fixed?

Prices are our general set prices for a vehicle in decent condition. Final pricing may vary depending on the condition, size and level of dirt of the vehicle. If your vehicle needs extra work, we will let you know before we start.

Where do you operate?

Brisbane is our core area, and we regularly service Logan, Ipswich, Redlands and Moreton Bay. Outside that, get in touch and we will confirm.

Do I need to bring my car anywhere?

No. We come to your home or workplace and complete the detail on site.

How does booking work?

Tell us about your vehicle, location and the service you want. We reply with a clear next step, then complete the detail at your location.

Is ceramic coating right for every car?

It depends on the vehicle's condition and preparation. We will tell you honestly if it is the right fit before booking it in.

What do I need to have ready?

It depends on the service and location. We will let you know exactly what is needed before your appointment.
